Hunny Partner Program

Partner terms

Effective August 11, 2026

Referral offer

A valid partner referral gives a new eligible customer 50% off their first Hunny Plus Monthly payment. It cannot be combined with another parent or partner referral.

Commissions and free access

Active partners earn CA$2 for each verified referred customer who starts a paid subscription. Commissions and free-month rewards are held for 14 days for refunds and fraud review. Partners earn one free Plus month after their first successful paid referral and another free month after every 10 additional successful paid referrals.

Payouts

Partners may request payout when their available balance reaches CA$10. Hunny will arrange the payout method and may request identity, payment, or tax information before processing it.

Fair promotion

Partners must use truthful content, disclose the affiliate relationship where required, and must not use self-referrals, fake accounts, spam, misleading claims, trademark impersonation, or unauthorized paid ads using Hunny brand terms. Refunded, fraudulent, duplicated, or policy-violating conversions may be voided.

Program changes

Hunny may suspend abusive accounts or change future commission terms with notice. Properly earned, non-void commissions remain payable under the terms that applied when they were earned.

Privacy and support

Partner data is used to operate referral tracking, prevent fraud, communicate about the program, and make payouts. See the Privacy Policy or contact support@hunnyappai.com.
